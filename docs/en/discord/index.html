Cubicase on Discord

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The Cubicase bot shows on your Discord whether the server is on and who is playing, announces when someone joins, pins the step-by-step for friends to get in and, with Cubicase Plus, lets the people you choose start, stop and back up the server without opening the computer.

1. What the bot does

  • Live status: a message that updates itself with on/off, online players, version and a join button.
  • Alerts: “server online”, “stopped”, “went down” and who joined and left (grouped, so the channel isn't flooded). You turn each type on or off.
  • How to join: a pinned message with the step-by-step and the server link, so friends don't have to ask.
  • Control from Discord (Cubicase Plus): start, stop, restart, back up, manage the whitelist and, if you enable it, send commands to the console.

2. How to link the bot to your Discord

  1. In Cubicase, open the server, go to Settings and find the Discord card. Click Add the bot and authorize it on Discord (you need the Manage Server permission there).
  2. Still on the card, click Generate code. It is valid for 10 minutes and works once.
  3. On Discord, someone with Manage Server types /cubicase link and pastes the code.
  4. Pick where the status, alerts and “how to join” go: on the card in the app (channel menus) or on Discord with /cubicase channel. Use Send test message to check.

That's it: the host's app must be open for the status and alerts to follow the server.

4. Free and Cubicase Plus

  • Free: live status, alerts and “how to join”, for one server in one Discord.
  • Cubicase Plus: control of the server from Discord (start, stop, restart, backup, whitelist and console), several Discords per server and several servers per Discord.
  • If Plus ends, anything beyond the free plan keeps working for 7 days and is then paused, not deleted. When Plus returns, everything comes back.

5. Bot permissions

The bot asks only for what it needs to post and pin messages: view channels, send messages, embed links, read history and pin messages. It does not ask for Administrator and does not read your Discord's conversations: it only reacts to /cubicase commands and writes in the channels you choose.

6. Privacy and how to remove it

  • We store the identifiers of your Discord and the chosen channels, and your preferences. Names of online players are only sent while a Discord is linked and are used to announce who joined and left. Details in the Privacy Policy.
  • To remove: /cubicase unlink, the Unlink button on the app's card, or kick the bot from your Discord.

7. Common problems

  • “I couldn't write in the channel”: run /cubicase diagnostics in the channel. It tells you whether the token, server and channel are right; the most common cause is a channel or category permission rule blocking the bot.
  • “The host's Cubicase is closed”: the app must be open on the host's computer to start, stop and back up.
  • “Part of Cubicase Plus”: Plus is checked on the account that linked the server. If you switched accounts, open the Discord card signed in with the Plus account and click Generate code.
  • Big backups: the command's message is updated when the backup finishes (it can take a few minutes).
