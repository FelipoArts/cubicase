Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Summary: Cubicase works without an account, has no ads and uses no behavioral analytics. Your server and worlds stay on your computer. Below is everything that goes through our infrastructure, and why.

1. Who is responsible

Cubicase is an independent project, developed and operated in Brazil (“we”). We are the controller of the data described here under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and, where applicable, comparable laws.

Privacy contact and exercise of rights: through the in-app Help Center (Help → Send message) or by e-mail at felipe@cubicase.net.

2. What stays on your computer

Servers, worlds, backups, mods, app settings, the app's technical log (cubeforge_debug.log) and the owner secret of your servers stay in folders on your computer (Documents/CubicaseServers, Documents/CubicaseBackups and the app data folder). We do not upload these files. The technical log only leaves your computer if you copy and send it with a help request.

4. Who we share it with (processors and third parties)

We do not sell data and we show no advertising. We use these providers to deliver the service; each handles data under its own policy:

  • Cloudflare — Cubicase API, storage of server records and the remote panel.
  • Supabase — accounts, subscriptions and remote panel data.
  • Stripe — payments (subscriptions and donations).
  • Resend — delivery of Help Center e-mails to us.
  • Oracle Cloud — backup (relay) server in São Paulo, used only when a direct connection is not possible; it sees the IP addresses of whoever uses it and the volume relayed, but not the content (the connection is encrypted).
  • Google (STUN servers) — used to discover the public IP address needed for the direct connection; Google sees the IP of whoever queries.
  • GitHub — hosting of the website and of the app installers/updates (the app checks published versions).
  • Mojang/Microsoft, Adoptium, PaperMC, Forge/NeoForge, Fabric — where the app downloads the server, Java and installers; they see the IP of whoever downloads.
  • Modrinth and CurseForge — mod search and download; they receive the search terms and, to identify mods, file hash codes (never the files).
  • Google Fonts — fonts for the website pages (Google sees the IP of whoever opens the pages).

We may also disclose data when legally required or ordered by a competent authority.

5. Legal bases (LGPD, art. 7)

Performance of a contract (providing the app and the features you asked for, including the subscription), legitimate interest (security, abuse and fraud prevention, and basic network operation) and compliance with a legal obligation (for example, tax records of the subscription). Where required we ask for your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

6. How long we keep it

  • Connection credentials and sessions: expire within 24 hours.
  • Monthly backup-server usage (bytes relayed per server): up to 40 days.
  • Per-IP anti-abuse counters: about 1 minute.
  • Server record: until you delete the server in the app or ask for removal.
  • Account: as long as it exists; you may ask for deletion.
  • Subscription data: while the subscription exists and for the period required by law for tax records.
  • Remote panel: temporary console content disappears when the app disconnects; the device registration stays until you remove it.
  • Help Center messages: as long as needed to handle and document the request.

7. International transfers

The providers above operate servers outside Brazil. By using the service your data may be processed in other countries, under the providers' own contractual safeguards and in line with the LGPD (art. 33).

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS and network encryption), store each server's owner secret only as a hash, restrict access to credentials and apply usage limits against abuse. No system is fully secure; if an incident may affect you, we will notify you as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You may ask for: confirmation that we process your data; access; correction; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed unlawfully; portability; information about who we share data with; deletion of data processed on the basis of consent; and withdrawal of consent. You may also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD). Use the contacts in section 1; we may ask you to confirm your identity.

10. Children and teenagers

Basic use of the app requires no personal data. Accounts, subscriptions and the remote panel are intended for people aged 18 or over; minors must use them with the participation and authorization of a legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what is needed to operate; if you are responsible for a child and believe they sent us data, contact us so we can remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The update date is at the top; relevant changes will be highlighted in the app or on the website.
